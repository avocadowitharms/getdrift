Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

drift is designed as a local-first workspace for project context. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use the website, join the waitlist, or contact support.

Information You Provide

If you subscribe for updates or contact us, we may collect your email address and any message details you choose to send. Waitlist subscriptions are handled through MailerLite.

Local-First Product Data

drift is built around local project workspaces. Project tasks, notes, files, repository context, and related workspace data are intended to stay on your device unless you choose to connect or sync with another service.

Website And Analytics

The website may receive standard technical information such as browser type, device information, referring pages, and basic request logs through hosting and third-party services.

How Information Is Used

  • To send product updates or waitlist messages.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the website and product experience.

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted service providers for email subscriptions, hosting, and support workflows. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services.

Your Choices

You can unsubscribe from waitlist emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in those messages. You can also contact us to request help with privacy-related questions.
